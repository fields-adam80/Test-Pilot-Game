EXPERIMENTAL FLIGHT PROGRAM

You are a test pilot flying an experimental prototype through a orbital high-speed maneuverability course.

Each illuminated test barrier has a precision opening — rectangular, circular, or triangular. The wall and its opening appear small in the distance and grow larger as you approach. Track each moving aperture against the barrier grid, line up the prototype, and thread the opening. Some stay centered; others move left, right, up, or down from that shared starting point.

Touch anywhere on the game and drag your finger to steer. Drag your finger to steer the prototype. It banks and responds directly to your movement.

Line yourself up and pass through. The walls come quickly — clear one and immediately read the next.
